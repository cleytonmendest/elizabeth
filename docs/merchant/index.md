## How long it takes

With a catalog already in place, a store is configured in **1 to 2 hours** following the getting-started guide. What usually takes longer isn't the theme — it's gathering photos and writing copy.

## What you need first

- A Shopify store (paid plan or trial)
- Your logo as **PNG or SVG with a transparent background**
- Product photos, ideally **all in the same aspect ratio** — the guide explains why
- Your brand colors, if you have a defined palette. If you don't, the theme ships with one that works.
