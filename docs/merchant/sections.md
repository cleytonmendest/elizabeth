# 3. Sections, one by one

The theme has **23 sections you add wherever you want**. On top of those, the **header**, the **footer** and **10 page sections** come already in place — you adjust them, you don't add them.

All of them have **Color scheme** — see [Colors and brand](colors-and-brand.html).

---

## How to add one

In the editor: sidebar → **Add section** → pick it by name.

Sections that accept **blocks** (repeated items, like each testimonial or each column) show **Add block** once you click into them.

---

## Header and footer

### Announcement Bar
A scrolling strip at the top. One or several messages that cycle.

**Use it for:** free shipping above X, delivery times, the coupon of the week.

Configures: cycle duration, gap between messages, direction, pause on hover. Each message is a block.

> Three messages at most. With more, nobody reads any of them.

### Header
The site header. Can't be added or removed.

Configures: main menu, desktop layout type, sticky header. Accepts a **Mega menu image** block.

### Footer
The site footer. Can't be added or removed.

Configures: newsletter (with its own color scheme), language selector, country/currency selector. Blocks for **Sub-links** (the link columns) and **Payments and Social**.

## Home page — visual impact

### Image banner
A large image with text and a button on top.

**Use it for:** the top of the home page, announcing a new collection.

Configures: image, alt text, height, eyebrow, heading, body, content position, **scrim** and opacity, button and link, full width.

> **Scrim** is the dark layer between the image and the text. Without it, white text over a light photo is unreadable. If the text disappears into the photo, raise the scrim opacity before changing the text color.

### Image Slider
Several images in a carousel.

**Use it for:** showing more than one highlight in the same space.

Configures: infinite loop, arrows, dots, how many images per screen on desktop/tablet/mobile, autoplay and interval, pause on hover. Each image is a block.

> Autoplay that's too fast is irritating. 5 seconds is a sensible minimum.

### Video
Video hosted on Shopify or from YouTube/Vimeo, with text on top.

Configures: display mode, the video, poster image, height, eyebrow, heading, body, position, scrim, button.

> The **poster image** is what shows before the video loads. Without it the section is grey for the first moments.

### Highlighted Section
Image and text side by side, with an optional decorative curved background.

**Use it for:** telling your brand story, introducing a line.

Configures: alignment, curved background (with its own color scheme). **Image** and **Text** blocks.

### Images-Link Section
A grid of images that lead to links.

**Use it for:** visual categories — "Dresses", "Tops", "Sale".

### Shop the Look
One photo with **pins** over the products in it.

**Use it for:** a complete look, where the shopper buys the pieces in the photo.

Configures: image, alt text, heading, body. Each **Product pin** is a block.

> Pins are positioned by coordinates. If you swap the photo, reposition all of them.

## Home page — products

### Featured collection
A product grid from one collection.

Configures: collection, heading, subheading, alignment, desktop display (grid or carousel), columns, max products, second image on hover, quick-add button.

### Cards Slider
A carousel of free-form cards — image, text and link you compose yourself.

**Use it for:** things that aren't products: categories, benefits, posts.

### Product recommendations
Related products, chosen by Shopify.

**Use it on:** the product page, below the description.

### Recently viewed
The products the shopper opened herself.

**Use it on:** the product page or the cart. On a new store it stays empty until there's browsing.

### Highlighted Product
A single product with its full detail — price, variants, buy button.

**Use it for:** a launch, an anchor product.

## Home page — trust and content

### Customer Testimonials
A carousel of customer quotes.

Configures: section scheme **and** card scheme, heading, subheading, autoplay, speed, arrows. Each testimonial is a block.

### Trust Badges
A strip of badges — shipping, returns, secure payment, installments.

Configures: mobile layout. Each badge is a block.

### Multicolumn
Two to four columns with icon, heading and text.

**Use it for:** benefits, steps, differentiators.

### Rich text
Text only: eyebrow, heading, paragraph and button.

**Use it for:** a brand manifesto, a notice, a call to action.

### FAQ / Collapsible content
Questions that expand and collapse.

**Use it for:** the FAQ page, delivery questions, a size chart.

Configures: eyebrow, heading, open the first item. Each question is a block.

### Countdown Timer
A clock counting down to a date.

Configures: countdown type, date and time, show days, number style, alignment, full width, eyebrow, heading, body, image, scrim, button.

> **When the clock hits zero, the section disappears from the store** — it stays visible in the editor, so you can reconfigure it. Schedule the end together with the real end of the promotion, or the page is left with a hole.

### Blog Posts
Your latest posts.

Configures: blog, number of posts, columns, excerpt, author, tags, "view all" button.

### Newsletter Modal
An email capture popup.

Configures: enable, width, image, heading, body, button, delay, show on scroll, exit intent, don't show again for N days, "no thanks" link, success and error messages.

> The modal is the section that annoys people most when misconfigured. Delay of at least **10 seconds**, "don't show again" of at least **7 days**, and always keep the dismiss link visible.

### Apps
A slot for blocks from apps installed on the store.

## Fixed pages

These come already in place on the page they belong to. You adjust them; you don't need to add them.

> **Main Blog** and **Blog Article** also show up in the *Add section* list. Adding them to another page shows nothing useful — they read the blog or the post of the page they're on.

| Section | Page | Main options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product** | product | media width and layout, zoom, mobile layout; blocks for price, variant, quantity, stock, description, collapsible rows, badges, payment methods |
| **Collection Page** | collection | products per page and per row, second image on hover, collection image and description, **filters**, sorting, "load more" |
| **Collections list** | /collections | heading, collections per row and per page, product count |
| **Cart** | cart | continue shopping, order notes, checkout button text |
| **Search Page** | search | results per page, filter by type, suggestions when empty, popular searches |
| **Main Blog** | blog | articles per page, columns, tag filter, sidebar with search/categories/recent |
| **Blog Article** | post | featured image, author, tags, sharing, bio, related, prev/next, comments, sidebar |
| **Page** | pages | show title, text width |
| **Contact** | contact | show title, supporting text, ask for phone |
| **404 page** | 404 | search field, button text |
| **Password-protected store** | password | ask for the email of people who want to be notified |
| **Style guide (internal)** | internal | — not for shoppers; it's there to check colors |
